Kia ora e te whānau,

Here are this week's whānau notices.

## Koroneihana — Saturday 5 September

We are leaving Tain's at **8.30am** and driving to Hopuhopu.

To get there, take the Taupiri turn-off and head towards Ngaruawahia. The Hopuhopu base is on the right. The turn-off for Hopuhopu Base Road is on the right at the bridge as you leave Taupiri.

There is free parking at Hopuhopu, and you can catch a free bus to the marae. The buses run at 15-minute intervals.

We expect to be back in Auckland at about **4pm**, leaving the marae at about **2.45pm**.

Please bring warm clothes.

If you would like to join us, please call **021 112 9529**.

## Whakatau for Minister Ivicia — Friday 11 September

The whakatau for Minister Ivicia will be held at the marae at **10am**.

Afterwards, we will have a cup of tea and bikkies, or head to the café.
